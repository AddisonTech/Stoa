# Stoa

AI trading agents that tell you what to buy and when, graded against just holding SPY.

Stoa runs once a day. A team of roles proposes trades, a rule-based risk engine has the final word, and every decision is logged with its reasoning and scored against two baselines. It proves itself in backtest, on a sealed holdout and in live paper trading before it ever touches real money.
